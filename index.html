How is my food donation used?

It's matched to a verified NGO or individual by capacity and picked up by an e-bike driver before it expires.

Where does donated money go?

Directly to delivery costs and essentials kits — Second Plate is a non-profit, so no cut is kept.

How are NGOs verified?

Through registration checks and proof-of-delivery videos shared after each drop-off.

Second Plate is a non-profit prototype. Deliveries run on electric bikes to cut fuel costs and emissions — we're working toward free government charging or our own solar charging points.
